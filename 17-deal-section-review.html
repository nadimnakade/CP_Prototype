§5 Borrower Analysis

The borrower, Riverbend Developments Pty Ltd, is a special purpose vehicle (SPV) established solely for the Riverbend Rise project. The parent entity, Riverbend Group, has a 15-year track record in residential development across South-East Queensland.

5.1 Borrower Background

The Riverbend Group has completed 14 residential projects since 2009, with a total delivered value exceeding $380 million. The group's principals, Michael Chen and Sarah Williams, each bring over 20 years of experience in property development and construction finance.

5.2 Financial Capacity

The borrower's equity contribution of $2.4M (18.5% of total project cost) demonstrates meaningful skin in the game. Group net assets stand at $12.8M as at the most recent audited accounts (30 June 2024).

⚠ Issue: Contingency Analysis

The QS-recommended contingency of 10% appears conservative relative to the borrower's track record. However, current market conditions warrant higher contingency reserves. Recommend maintaining 10% until completion of Stage 1.
