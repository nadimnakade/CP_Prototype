Riverbend Rise

Construction Facility
Brisbane, QLD Residential $7.75m 14 months

1. Executive Summary

This paper assesses a $7.75 million, 14-month construction facility for Riverbend Rise, a boutique residential development comprising 24 apartments in Brisbane, QLD. The facility will fund construction costs with repayment from the sale of completed units.

The deal is supported by a well-located site, experienced sponsor, and conservative pre-sales. Key risks include construction cost escalation and market absorption risk.

2. Borrower and Sponsor

The borrower is Riverbend Developments Pty Ltd (ACN 12 345 678 901), a single purpose vehicle established for this development. The sponsor has a strong track record in residential projects across South East Queensland.

3. Property and Location

The property is located at 123 River Road, Brisbane QLD. The site comprises a 2,800 sqm parcel zoned for medium density residential, with existing development approval for 24 apartments.

4. Funding Request

The borrower requests a $7.75 million construction facility to fund the development of 24 apartments at 123 River Road, Brisbane. Drawdown is staged across 14 months with repayment from the sale of completed units.

5. Valuation Analysis

The as-is value is assessed at $7.0 million, with an as-if-complete value of $17.27 million. The development margin of 62.7% is considered strong for the asset class.
